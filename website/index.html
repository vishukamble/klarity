Root-Cause Extraction

No more digging through 30-line stack traces. klarity parses Java, Python, and Go logs to extract the exact error instantly.

⚡

Multi-Cluster Native

Go-routines scan your entire kubeconfig in parallel. Environments (prod, staging) are auto-detected by context naming.

🛡️

Strictly Read-Only

Perfectly safe for production. klarity only performs `get` and `list` operations.

⚙️

YAML Native

Simple, human-readable config that lives in your home directory.
